A FARM-TO-TABLE EXPERIENCE

From our soil to your table — Vaqrylen brings the freshest seasonal produce directly from our fields to your home. Every item is harvested at peak ripeness and delivered with care.

We cultivate heritage varieties using sustainable practices, honoring the land and the hands that tend it. Taste the difference that honest farming makes.

Our farm spans fifty acres of protected agricultural land just outside San Diego, where Mediterranean climate and rich alluvial soil create ideal growing conditions. We rotate crops seasonally, allowing the earth to regenerate naturally without synthetic inputs.

Every Tuesday and Friday, our harvest teams move through the rows at dawn, selecting only produce that meets our exacting standards. By noon, baskets are packed and en route to homes, restaurants, and our weekend farm stand.

We believe transparency builds trust. Visit us any Saturday to walk the fields, meet the growers, and taste the difference that three generations of stewardship brings to every bite.
